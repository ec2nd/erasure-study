Informed Consent for Participation in Research

You are invited to participate in a research study evaluating critical engagement with text. Your participation is completely voluntary.

Procedure: If you agree to participate, you will be asked to complete a brief pre-task survey, read a short stanza of text, perform an interactive erasure task, and answer a few post-task questions. The entire module should take approximately 5 minutes.

Risks & Benefits: There are no anticipated risks to participating in this study beyond those encountered in everyday life. While you may not experience direct benefits, your responses will contribute to our understanding of human-AI interaction.

Confidentiality: Your responses are collected anonymously. No personally identifying information will be linked to your survey answers or your task interactions.

Right to Withdraw: You are free to decline to participate or to withdraw from the study at any time by closing your browser window.

By clicking "I Agree" below, you confirm that you are at least 18 years of age, have read the information provided, and voluntarily consent to participate.

Erasure Task Instructions

In the following module, you will be presented with a short stanza of poetry generated by an AI model based on a photograph.

Please read the text carefully. You may click on any word to "black it out" and permanently redact it from the stanza. Your objective is to leave behind a new, altered poem.

If you change your mind, you can click a blacked-out word again to restore it. Once you are satisfied with your final erasure, click the "Lock Erasure" button to finalize your choices.
